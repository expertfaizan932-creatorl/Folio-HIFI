Folio Agent - End User Licence Agreement
=========================================

This software is provided to employees of Hifi Marketing for the purpose of
recording working time. Installing it means you accept the terms below.

1. WHAT THE AGENT RECORDS
   The agent records your working hours, idle time, break time, the applications
   and websites you use, and periodic screenshots. It is installed so that your
   employer can report accurate working time.

2. TRANSPARENCY
   The agent has a visible tray icon whenever it is running. It is never hidden.
   What was recorded is visible to you, in the agent window and on the company
   website. Hiding it is not possible and is not permitted.

3. YOUR DATA
   Data is stored on the company server and belongs to your employer. It is kept
   in line with the company's retention policy. Local data is stored under your
   Windows profile in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WorkFolioAgent and is removed when you
   uninstall.

4. REMOVAL
   You may uninstall Folio Agent from Windows Settings at any time. The agent
   supports a single undo for its activities: the actions it takes are limited
   to reading your screen and keystroke activity, sending them to the company
   server, and starting itself with Windows.

6. PRIVACY
   Handling of the recorded data is governed by the company's privacy notice.
